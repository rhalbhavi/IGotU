Support, made easier to find.

IGotU helps people in communities and public spaces connect with the local, regional, and national support services and trusted contacts they need in situations of emergency or distress.

For organizations

One platform, dedicated organization pages

Organizations can configure a dedicated support page for their residential community, educational campus, office, or other location. Each page has its own stable link and QR code while using the same IGotU experience.

Location pages are shared directly by the organization, for example through its QR code. This overview does not publish a directory of organization pages.

Verified organization admins can sign in to add and maintain local security, medical, and counsellor contacts. They can publish their page and download its QR code from the dashboard.

Important to know

Support-page call links open the visitor's device phone app with the listed number, allowing them to place a call directly to that service. IGotU itself does not place or receive calls, notify responders, or dispatch help. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, use the emergency call link or dial your local emergency number from your phone.

Published contact details are public to anyone who has the page link. Organizations are responsible for verifying their numbers, service hours, and response procedures.
