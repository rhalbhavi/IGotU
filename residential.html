    Important information

    IGotU lists ways to contact emergency services, local support, mental health helplines, or someone you trust. Tapping a call link opens your device's phone app with the listed number so you can place the call. IGotU itself does not place or receive calls, or dispatch responders.

    If you or someone else is in immediate danger, use the 112 call link or dial 112 from your phone. Your device must be able to place the call; IGotU does not automatically notify emergency services.

    This site does not request or use your device location. Calls and messages are initiated by you; review the recipient and message before sending.

    Helpline availability and local service coverage may change. If a number does not connect, use your local emergency service or another available support option.
